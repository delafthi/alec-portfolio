---
title: About
---

ALEC was born in 1961 in Zurich. Since childhood he has been passionate about
art and taught himself to draw autodidactically.

At age 15 he passed the entrance examination for the Zurich Art School, but
chose instead to complete a three-year apprenticeship in advertising and
marketing, followed by a career in banking.

After leaving banking, he dedicated himself fully to painting. He studied under
master painter Henrik Frei Larsson and trained for several years with Mariana
Scvortova. He also attended workshops at the Franz Gertsch Museum in Burgdorf/BE
on woodcut (Franz Gertsch), painting by Varlin, and the work of Xenia Hausner.

His work spans naturalistic landscapes, animals, and portraits across a wide
range of techniques: pencil, charcoal, chalk, pastel, acrylic, and oil on
canvas.

His artistic influences include Swiss painters Franz Gertsch, Cuno Amiet, and
Rolf Ziegler, as well as Brazilian painter Harding Meyer. He has been
particularly moved by the work of Xenia Hausner, whose portraits impressed him
through their intensity of expression and technical mastery.

Alec has played classical piano since the age of seven. He later developed a
passion for jazz improvisation and blues.
